About Me — Mustapha Gbanie

Al-salamu alaukum & hello! I’m Mustapha Gbanie, the person behind Zyvora

I’ve always loved creating things and solving problems — and I fell in love with building websites because it lets me help people turn their beautiful ideas into something real that the whole world can see. I’m based in Kenema, Sierra Leone, and I treat every single project like it’s something I’m making for myself.

For me, this is more than just work: it’s about being kind, listening well, and making sure you feel comfortable and understood from the very first chat until your site is live and working perfectly. I don’t use confusing words or rush you — I explain everything simply, and I will keep working until you are 100% happy with what we make together.

If you are new to this, don’t worry at all! I will guide you through every part. If you already know what you want — I will help you make it even better.

Thank you so much for stopping by my page. You can ask me anything, share any idea you have, or just message me to say hi — I am always here, and I can’t wait to work with you. Let’s make something amazing, insha’Allah
