Privacy Policy

At Zyvora, your privacy and trust mean everything to us. This page is here to explain simply how we look after any information you share with us, so you always feel safe and comfortable when you visit or connect with us

What information do we collect?

We only collect details that you choose to give us yourself — like your name, phone number, email address, or what you share when you send a message or ask for our help. We never take any personal details without your permission. We do not see or keep any private information that you do not choose to share with us.

How do we use your information?

We use your details only to reply to your messages, answer your questions, talk to you about your project, and give you the best service we can. We will never send you unwanted messages, and we will never share, sell, or give your personal details to anyone else for any reason. Your information stays safe with us, and only us.

How we keep your information safe

We take good care to protect everything you share with us. We use safe ways to store details, and we only keep them for as long as we need to help you. If you ever ask us to remove your information from our records, we will do that right away, no questions asked.

External links

Sometimes we may share links to other helpful websites or pages. Please know that once you click and go to those sites, their own privacy rules will apply. We always try to share only trusted and safe links, but we do not control what happens on those other sites.

Updates to this note

If anything changes in how we work, we will update this page here so you always know exactly how we look after you. You are welcome to check this page anytime.

If you have any question at all about your privacy or how we handle your details, please just message us — we will explain everything clearly and happily help you.
