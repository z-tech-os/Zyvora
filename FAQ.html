Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the things people ask most often. If you do not see what you are looking for here, just message me anytime — I will be so happy to answer you personally

1. What is Zyvora?

Zyvora is my friendly tech brand where I build websites, make documents, presentations, and give tech help for anyone who wants to bring their ideas online.

2. Who is behind Zyvora?

My name is Mustapha Gbanie — I am a web developer based in Kenema, Sierra Leone, and I run everything here with care.

3. What services do you offer?

I build all kinds of websites, do Microsoft Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, and give general tech support.

4. Can you make a website that works on phones too?

Yes! Every site I build is made to look and work perfectly on phones, tablets, laptops and computers.

5. Do I need to know anything about technology to work with you?

Not at all! I explain everything in simple words, and I guide you through every part step by step.

6. How much will my project cost?

It depends on what you need, how big it is, and what features you want. I will give you a clear price once we talk about your idea, and it will always be fair.

7. Can we pay in parts instead of all at once?

Yes, we can agree on a payment plan that works well and is easy for you.

8. How long will it take to finish my website?

Simple sites can be ready in a few days, bigger ones take a little longer. I will tell you the time before we start.

9. Will I be able to see how it looks while you work?

Of course! I will show you updates along the way so you can tell me what you think.

10. Can I ask for changes after you finish it?

Yes! I want you to love what we make together, so I will adjust things until you are happy.

11. Do I own my website once it is done?

Yes — once everything is settled, the website and everything on it belongs fully to you.

12. Will my personal details be safe with you?

Absolutely. I never share your information with anyone else, and I only keep what I need to help you.

13. I have no idea what design to pick — can you help me?

Yes! I can show you simple, nice examples and help you choose what fits you best.

14. Can you add my social media pages to my website?

Yes, I can link all your pages like Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and more right on your site.

15. What if something stops working after you finish?

Just tell me, and I will fix it quickly for you.

16. Do you make websites for schools, churches or masjids?

Yes! I build sites for businesses, families, schools, organizations, NGOs and more.

17. Why should I choose you instead of someone else?

Because I work kindly, honestly, listen well, and treat every project like it is for someone in my own family.

18. Do you make documents and presentations too?

Yes — I make neat, professional Word documents and clear, nice PowerPoint slides for any use.

19. How do I start working with you?

Just message me on WhatsApp or email, tell me your idea, and we will take it from there simply together.

20. What if I have a question that is not here?

Please just ask me! I am always happy to answer anything you want to know, anytime.
