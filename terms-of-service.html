Terms of Service

Welcome to Zyvora! These simple terms are here so we both understand how we work together, to make sure everything goes smoothly, fairly, and kindly for all of us

About our services

I build websites, create documents, make presentations, and give tech support to help bring your ideas to life. All work is done with care, skill, and my full attention, to match what we agree together.

How we work together

  1. When we start a project, we will talk clearly about what you want, what it will include, and when we aim to finish it.
  2. You will have the chance to see updates and give your thoughts, and I will adjust things until you are happy with the result.
  3. All ideas, designs, and work you share with me will only be used for your project and nothing else.

Payments and agreements

If there is any payment agreed for work, we will talk about this openly before we start. We can arrange it in a way that works well for both of us. I will never ask for more than what we agreed together.

Your responsibilities

Please share all the details, pictures, or information needed for your project, and let me know your feedback in good time so we can finish nicely as planned. Please only give me content that you own or have permission to use.

My responsibilities

I will work honestly, keep your details private, and keep you updated on how things are going. If anything comes up that might change our plan, I will tell you straight away so we can sort it out together.

Ownership of work

Once everything is fully agreed and settled, the final work made for you belongs to you. I may keep a copy of it as an example of my work unless you tell me you would prefer I do not.

Changes and updates

I may update these terms sometimes, and any changes will be shown on this page. If anything important changes for an ongoing project we are doing, I will let you know personally.

If anything here is not clear to you, please just ask — I am happy to explain it in simple words that make sense to you.
